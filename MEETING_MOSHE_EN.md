> "The benchmark number is not the contribution. Reproducing RadioML accuracy
> is solved. What I am measuring is *why* a classifier that reaches 99.9% on the

## 1. WHAT WE HAVE — measured, with the file to show for it

### 1.1 The domain-gap table — the headline result

`compare_methods_ICRNNA_es.npz` · ICRNNA · 5 shared classes (BPSK, QPSK, 8PSK,
16QAM, 64QAM) · 5 seeds · 60-epoch ceiling / patience 20

| method | in-domain | cross-domain | gap | 16QAM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 0.999 | **0.804** ±0.005 | **+0.195** | **0.060** |
| literature augmentation | 1.000 | 0.806 ±0.003 | +0.194 | 0.036 |
| **whitening α=0.75** | 1.000 | **0.993** ±0.001 | **+0.007** | **0.991** |
| whitening + augmentation | 0.999 | 0.994 ±0.003 | +0.005 | 0.998 |

> "A model at 0.999 in-domain falls to 0.804 on an independently generated
> domain, and 16QAM — the class the whole diagnosis centres on — collapses to
> 0.060. The augmentation set from the literature, rotation and conjugate flip
> and additive noise, moves that gap by 0.001. Spectral whitening moves it from
> nineteen points to less than one, and costs nothing in-domain."

*Note:* the table is **complete at 20 of 20** and the file is in the
repository. But the fourth row beats the third by **+0.001**, which is not a
measurement. Adding the literature augmentation on top of whitening
contributes nothing; whitening is doing all of the work. That is the opposite
of what the file's own docstring predicted, and the prediction was written
before the measurement, so it stays as a refuted hypothesis.

### 1.2 Plain classification — evidence the backbone is competent

| run | overall | SNR ≥ 10 dB | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| RML2016.10a, 11 classes, 3 seeds | **0.6223** ±0.0019 | **0.9148** ±0.0013 | converged (best epoch 39) |
| RadioML 2018.01A, 24 classes, 3 seeds | **0.5699** ±0.0081 | **0.8716** ±0.0141 | converged (best epoch 26) |

For 2018, 512 of the 4096 frames per (class, SNR) cell, at the native
1024-sample frame length.

> "These are not the contribution — they exist so that the cross-domain numbers
> are known to start from a competent classifier rather than a broken one."

*Note:* almost all of the 2016 error is **one cell**. WBFM is read as AM-DSB
60% of the time, and that single cell is 64% of every misclassified frame at
SNR ≥ 10 dB. Drop WBFM and the other ten classes sit at 0.968. This is a
property of the dataset, not of the model — both classes are generated from
voice recordings, and during the silences in the source audio both degrade to
an unmodulated carrier.

### 1.3 Against the published paper — the reproduction question is closed

`colab/icrnna_faithful_2016.py`, built from the paper (El-Haryqy et al.,
*Results in Engineering* 26 (2025) 104783) rather than from a reproduction:

| | 58-epoch ceiling (the paper's own budget) | 150-epoch ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| best epoch | 58, 58, 55 — **at the ceiling** | **107** — early stopping decided |
| test accuracy | 61.75% ±0.07 | **63.21%** |
| paper: 63.24% | −1.49 points | **−0.03 points** |
| parameters | 794,827 (paper: 0.79M) ✓ | same |

> "The faithful build reproduces the paper — 63.21 against 63.24. It needed
> nearly twice the published epoch count to get there, and that was the whole of
> the deficit. The paper's 'Number of Epochs: 58' is a report of where its own
> early stopping landed, not a recipe that transfers."

### 1.4 A methodological finding — worth raising

**Convergence is set by gradient updates, not epochs.** 2016 converged in
23,439 updates and 2018 in 22,672 — within 3% of each other, despite differing
in class count and in sequence length by a factor of eight.

But that is not a constant. Inside `compare_methods` the stopping epoch depends
strongly on the **method**:

| method | stopping epochs | mean | updates |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | 51, 41, 48, 36, 38 | 43 | 9.8k – 13.9k |
| standard augmentation | 89, 48, 83, 65, 82 | 73 | 13.1k – 24.3k |
| whitening α=0.75 | 26, 26, 33 | 28 | 7.1k – 9.0k |

> "Whitening converges 2.6× faster than the augmentation row. That is what the
> mechanism predicts — whitening removes a nuisance dimension so there is less
> to fit; augmentation adds one so there is more. It also means a single epoch
> ceiling cannot be assumed safe across a sweep whose cells differ in method."

### 1.5 Causal attribution — four refuted hypotheses, one working intervention

The gap concentrates almost entirely in one cell: 16QAM read as 64QAM. Four
explanations were **refuted** by controlled experiment:

| hypothesis | outcome |
|---|---|
| occupied bandwidth differs | refuted — matching bandwidth did not recover 16QAM |
| constellation density | refuted — density is not what the model reads |
| symbol-timing regularity | refuted — RadioML has **no** symbol-rate spectral line |
| channel impairments (CFO, multipath, IQ imbalance) | refuted — removing them one at a time changed little |

What worked: **phase-preserving magnitude substitution**
(`narrow_the_search.py`). Take a frame from the failing domain, keep its phase
spectrum exactly, and substitute only the magnitude spectrum of the training
domain. The prediction follows the magnitude spectrum.

> "That is an intervention on the signal, not an observation of the
> representation — so it supports a causal claim rather than a correlational
> one. The cause is the transmitter's spectral envelope, chiefly its
> pulse-shaping roll-off."

*Note:* these are signal-level and independent of the classifier, so they still
stand. But the **accuracy numbers attached to them are pending
re-measurement.**

### 1.6 Other valid results that involve no model

- `baseline_results.npz` — the classical cumulant + SVM pipeline, 11
  modulations, peak accuracy 0.974. No neural network, still valid.
- `sink_vs_geometry.npz` — signal geometry, no model, still valid.
- `overfit_2x2.json` — architecture comparison (IQNet vs ICRNNA × fixed vs
  plateau LR), still valid.
- The **signal-level** figures in `figures/` (spectrograms, constellations,
  class spectra) are valid. Anything showing an accuracy curve or a confusion
  matrix **predates the current backbone — do not use it.**

- The cost is small: **the ceiling only matters to a cell that hits it.** A cell
  that peaked at epoch 36 peaks at 36 whatever ceiling it ran under. Ten cells
  rerun, not twenty.

## 4. CAVEATS TO STATE UP FRONT

Do not make him find these. Say them. The credibility comes from here.

1. **The current model is not the published architecture.**
   `model_zoo.ICRNNA` was transcribed from a peer's reproduction; against the
   paper it differs in **five places** (conv1 kernel 5 vs 3, two max-pools vs
   one, one BatchNorm after the LSTM stack vs one per layer, no attention
   dropout or LayerNorm, one dense layer of 128 at dropout 0.5 vs two of 128 and
   64 at 0.3). Do not call it "the published architecture". The faithful build
   is a separate file and **does** reproduce the paper (§1.3).

2. **Whitening is not novel.** WhiteNet (arXiv:2608.06581) arrived at
   essentially the same operation, and on **real** over-the-air captures. The
   novelty claim was dropped on finding it. What survives: the attribution
   method, the partial-vs-full disagreement, and the sink finding. It is
   recorded in `LITERATURE.md`.

3. **The mechanism is not fully resolved.** The working account through most of
   the project was shortcut learning: the model reads modulation order off the
   spectral envelope. **Two experiments killed it** — most directly the envelope
   transplant, where handing 16QAM frames a 64QAM envelope left the prediction
   at 16QAM. The envelope does not *carry* the class; removing it still helps.
   That contradiction is stated **as unresolved**.

   > "I would rather tell you the part I cannot explain than have you find it."

4. **The small differences in the table are not measurements.** At a 60-epoch
   ceiling at least two cells stopped without converging. The 0.804 → 0.993
   headline is unaffected (19 points against a seed spread of 0.005). But
   differences the size of whitening vs whitening+augmentation (+0.001) or none
   vs augmentation (+0.002) are **not reportable** until the e100 table is
   finished.

5. **Three seeds is thin on 2018.** Seed spread reaches 0.021 on 24 classes. A
   difference of less than about 0.03 between two configurations measured this
   way **is not yet a difference.**

## 5. WHERE WE WANT HELP — what to ask Moshe

If anything is to come out of the meeting, it is these:

1. **SDR access.** "Both domains are synthetic" is the largest single-sentence
   hole. A USRP or HackRF, lab access, and guidance on the capture protocol —
   this is the one thing that moves the work from a study to a result.
2. **The open-set recognition literature.** That is the field adjacent to the
   sink finding, and a few searches do not cover it. The novelty question there
   is genuinely open. Who should I be reading?
3. **Which piece first?** Compute is limited. The α sweep (cheap, confirms the
   WhiteNet disagreement) or the 24-class leave-one-out (expensive, most novel)?
   An outside opinion is worth having here.
4. **Target venue.** The contribution is an attribution method, not a benchmark
   number. Does that belong in a signal processing venue or a machine learning
   one?
